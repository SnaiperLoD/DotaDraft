# Battle Engine

## Purpose

Compare user draft against saved professional teams.

## Input

Team A: User draft

Team B: Stored match draft

## Factors

Battle Engine may consider:

- hero matchups;
- role matchups;
- strategic conflicts;
- power spikes;
- scaling;
- objectives.

## Output

Returns:

- Win probability
- Advantages
- Disadvantages
- Explanation

## Important

Battle Engine does NOT use:
Draft Score

Evaluation Engine and Battle Engine are independent.
